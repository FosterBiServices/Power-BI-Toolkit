Step2

    

      

        
Your dates

        
Time intelligence needs a date table with one row per day for whole years, related to your facts. The Date Table Generator can build one.

      

      

        

        

        

        

        

        

      

      

      

        

        

      

      
The trend needs two columns of your date table: a month label such as Jan 2026 and a month number that runs on across years (Year × 12 + Month), to count back N months. Columns you don’t have are added as calculated columns.

      

      
The measures follow SQLBI’s standard time-related calculations pattern. The script adds hidden helpers to your date table: a DateWithSales column (TRUE up to the last date with data) and, for measures, a ShowValueForDates measure, so nothing shows after your data ends and last year stops at the same day.

      
The measures follow SQLBI’s month-related calculations pattern: they filter month columns instead of dates, so they work on a date table with one row per month (or per day, at month level). Columns you don’t have are added as hidden calculated columns.
