Model Documenter

Turn a Power BI project into one HTML document: tables, measures, lineage, relationships, sources, model checks and report pages. It reads your PBIP folder right here in the browser, or works from the saved model export.

Choose the folder that holds your .pbip file, or a .SemanticModel folder. The project must be saved as a Power BI Project (PBIP) with the TMDL format. The files are read on this page and never leave your computer, even if the browser’s prompt says “upload”.
