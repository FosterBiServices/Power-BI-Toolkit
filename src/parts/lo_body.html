Layout and visuals

Suggest a layout (3-30-3: answer a few questions, get three layouts)

A good report page answers three questions in order. 3 seconds: how are we doing? (headline KPIs top left, big numbers, status against target). 30 seconds: where and why? (trend, simple bar charts, heatmaps, filters). 300 seconds: which records? (details on demand: a formatted table or a drill-through page).

Wireframe with sample visuals

Background image

In Power BI Desktop: Format page > Canvas background > Image, Image fit: Fit, Transparency 0%. Then turn off each visual’s own background so the cards show through. The PNG is the page size you set in Step 1. PNG at 2× is twice that, for extra sharpness.

PowerPoint

Three slides at the page’s size: the layout as editable shapes (header, logo, title, cards and labels you can move, resize and recolor, with your shadow), the wireframe with sample visuals, and the background image. Good for design reviews and sign-off.

Into Figma

  1. Copy for Figma, then in Figma select a page and press Ctrl+V. The drawing arrives as one frame of editable shapes, with layers named Page, Header, Side-panel and Card-<visual>. Your logo and header image come along as images.
  2. Refine it there (shapes, icons, gradients). Shadows come through as SVG filters; if one doesn’t, add a Drop shadow effect with the same X, Y, Blur, Spread, color and opacity as in Step 3. Hide the sample visuals layer before exporting the background.
  3. Export the background frame as PNG at 1× (the page size) and set it as the canvas background in Power BI Desktop, as above.
