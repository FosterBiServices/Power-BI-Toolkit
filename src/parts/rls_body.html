Step2

    

      

        
Set up the roles

        
Each role is a group of people who see the same slice of data. Add rules to say which rows they see; rules on different tables all apply. A role with no rules sees everything.

      

      

        Which kind of rule?
        

          
    
            
  • Fixed values: everyone in the role sees the same rows, such as Region is West. One role per region, team or country.
    • 
            
  • User’s own rows: a column holds each person’s sign-in email (a sales rep on each customer, say), and people see the rows with their email. One role covers everyone.
    • 
            
  • Access table: a separate table lists who may see what (Email and Region, one row per pair). One role covers everyone, and access changes by editing the table, not the model.
    • 
            
  • Custom DAX: any TRUE/FALSE expression for each row of a table. FALSE () hides the whole table from the role.
    •

After you publish

          
    
            
  1. In the Power BI service, open the semantic model’s … menu > Security, pick each role and add people or security groups. Groups are easier to keep up to date.
    2. 
            
  2. Roles only restrict people with the Viewer role in the workspace, or who get the report through an app or a share. Workspace Admins, Members and Contributors see all the data.
    3. 
            
  3. Use Test as role on the same Security page to check a real person’s view before you tell people the report is ready.
