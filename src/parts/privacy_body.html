Data and privacy

Where the toolkit keeps what you type, paste and open, and who can see it. Short version: it stays in your browser, on your computer.

In short

  • Nothing is uploaded. Files, folders and pasted text are read on the page, in your browser. The toolkit has no server that receives them.
  • No AI calls, no tracking. There are no analytics, ads, sign-ins or AI services in the pages. A Copilot prompt only reaches Copilot when you paste or attach it yourself.
  • Saved on your computer only. What a tool remembers between visits is kept in this browser, for this site. It isn’t on any other computer or browser, and Foster BI Services can’t see it.
  • You can remove it any time. Clear entries on a tool, or Clear all pages below.

What the toolkit keeps

Most tools work on the design of a model, not its data. A model export lists table, column and measure names, formulas, descriptions and data source details. It holds no data rows, except small tables typed straight into the model or built by a formula.

What the pages connect to

  • GitHub Pages sends the pages, and the files they need, to your browser. It never receives what you do on them.
  • Google Fonts sends the fonts. It sees that a page was opened, and from which IP address, like any website you visit.
  • Nothing else. The pages run no code from other servers.

A few pages link to reference sites such as SQLBI. Nothing goes there unless you follow the link and paste something in yourself.

Keeping it safe

The toolkit is built so your work has nowhere to go but your own screen. The risks that remain are the ones that come with any website, and each has a simple answer.

  • Shared or unattended computers. This is the main one. Choose Clear all pages when you finish on a shared computer, use a private (InPrivate or Incognito) window, and lock your screen when you step away.
  • Browser extensions. Only install extensions your organization approves.
  • What you give Copilot. Prompts carry names and formulas, not data rows, unless you add them. Follow your organization’s AI policy, and only use AI tools it approves.
  • The code itself. Every library the pages use is served from this site, not from another server. The pages are tested so that nothing typed or pasted into them can run as code. The code is public, so your IT team can review it: github.com/FosterBiServices/Power-BI-Toolkit.

Clear your data

  • One tool: Clear entries at the top of the tool. The page stays empty after a reload.
  • Every tool: the button below, or Clear all pages in any tool’s Clear entries. It empties every tool and removes the saved model export, the earlier exports and the PBIP folder access.
  • From your browser: clearing this site’s data in your browser settings removes it all too.
