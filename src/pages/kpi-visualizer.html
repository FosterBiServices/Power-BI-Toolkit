Colors

        
Status colors are good, neutral and bad, the way Excel’s conditional formatting shows them. Card colors are everything else: background, text, lines and the accent. The previews above and the measures you build use them all. Change any color, or take them from your theme. An arrow always goes with a status color, so it never relies on color alone.
