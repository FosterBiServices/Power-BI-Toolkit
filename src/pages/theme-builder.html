Step2

Fine-tune

Change any data color. Don’t like a suggested one? Other options under it lists colors that fit instead. Then choose the page and visual backgrounds, text, good and bad colors and the font. Everything else in the theme follows from these.

Data colors (series colors, in order)

The ratio on each color is its contrast with the background it sits on: how much it stands out, from 1:1 (invisible) to 21:1 (black on white). 4.5:1 or more is readable as text, such as titles and labels. 3:1 to 4.5:1 is fine for bars, lines and slices, but too light for text. Under 3:1 is hard to see; keep it for big shapes or less important series.

Good, neutral and bad

Used for waterfall increases and decreases, KPI status, and conditional formatting. The default is the dark green, dark yellow and dark red text colors from Excel’s conditional formatting.

Combo chart bubble size

For a line and clustered column chart that shows a bubble with a label over each column. Power BI’s default marker is too small for that, so the theme sets it. Only markers you turn on change, and only in that chart type.
